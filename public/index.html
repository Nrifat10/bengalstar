About Us

Your Trusted Manpower Partner in the Kingdom

Bangla Star Co., Ltd specializes in supplying trained, verified workers to petrol stations across Saudi Arabia — from busy city stations to highway travel plazas.

We handle recruitment, training, documentation and deployment end-to-end, so station owners get reliable staff on time — without the hassle.

  • Licensed & fully compliant with Saudi labor law
  • Every worker vetted, medically tested & pre-trained
  • Arabic & English speaking supervisors on call
  • Free replacement guarantee for every worker
