About Bengal Star

The workforce behind the Kingdom's busiest stations

Bengal Star Limited Company is a specialist manpower supply company headquartered in Riyadh. Since 2014 we have recruited, trained and deployed thousands of workers for petrol stations, fuel retail networks and highway service centers across Saudi Arabia.

Every candidate passes our in-house training program before reaching your forecourt — pump operation, customer service, POS systems and health & safety. You get station-ready staff from day one, with all documents handled end-to-end.

  • Visa, Iqama, medical & insurance handled

FAQ

Answers before you ask

How fast can workers be deployed?

Standard roles are deployed within 7–14 days of an approved request. If you already have workers in the Kingdom, transfers can be faster. Replacement workers arrive within 72 hours.

What documents do you handle?

End-to-end: visa processing, Iqama issuance, medical examinations, health insurance and employment contracts — fully compliant with Saudi labor law.

Which regions do you cover?

All major cities — Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Makkah, Madinah and more — plus highway corridors and remote sites with rotation crews and housing.

Can we scale the team up or down?

Yes. Contracts are flexible — add workers for seasonal peaks or adjust headcount with 30 days' notice. No long lock-ins.

What roles can you supply?

Fuel attendants, car wash and detailing crews, bakala/store staff, cashiers, tire & oil change technicians, shift supervisors and site cleaners — plus custom roles recruited to your specification.

How does pricing work?

One transparent monthly rate per worker that covers salary, insurance and our management fee. You receive a written quote after a short requirements call — no hidden costs.
